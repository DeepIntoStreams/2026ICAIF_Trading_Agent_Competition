# Phases

All times are **GMT+8**. The live phase follows the U.S. equity market calendar (weekdays,
excluding market holidays); date adjustments are announced on the platform.

## Phase 1 - Development & Validation

- **START: 12:00 GMT+8, Oct 1, 2026**
- **END: 23:59 GMT+8, Oct 24, 2026**
- **DESCRIPTION:** The competition opens. Register (individually or as a team), download the
  training data and Starter Kit, and build your agent locally with the official engine. Submit
  to the **online validation environment** (held-out **2025** data, mirrors the official
  protocol) up to **10 times per day**. Validation scores are feedback only and **do not** count
  toward the final ranking.

## Phase 2 - Official Live Competition

- **START: market open, Oct 27, 2026**
- **END: 23:59 GMT+8, Nov 7, 2026**
- **DESCRIPTION:** The live phase, on real market data released each trading day. Each trading
  day: retrieve the day's observation, run your agent, and **submit your target weights before
  the daily deadline**; after close, actions are validated and executed at the **next market
  open**. The leaderboard updates once per trading day. Only the weights you submit are scored -
  your code stays with you.

## Results & Presentation

Final rankings are computed from the live phase (see **Evaluation**). Top-ranked teams provide
their code for the **reproducibility audit**; verified rankings are announced on **Nov 10,
2026**, and the top teams are invited to present their solutions at **ACM ICAIF 2026**.
