# Phases - platform configuration

## Phase 1 - Development & Validation

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Phase name** | Development & Validation |
| **Start date / time** | Oct 1, 2026 · 00:00 ET |
| **End date / time** | Oct 24, 2026 · 23:59 ET |
| **Associated task** | **[set on platform]** - ICAIF 2026 Trading Agent (validation) |
| **Public data** | **[set on platform]** - training data + validation environment |
| **Starting Kit** | **[set on platform]** - ICAIF 2026 Trading Agent Starter Kit |

**Description:** Register your team, download the training data and Starter Kit, and build your
agent. Submit to the validation environment to check that your agent connects to and runs on our
system - up to 10 times per day. Validation results are feedback only and do not count toward the
final ranking.

## Phase 2 - Official Live Competition

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Phase name** | Official Live Competition |
| **Start date / time** | Oct 26, 2026 · 00:00 ET (first trading day) |
| **End date / time** | Nov 6, 2026 · 23:59 ET (final trading day) |
| **Associated task** | **[set on platform]** - ICAIF 2026 Trading Agent (live) |
| **Public data** | **[set on platform]** - daily live observations |
| **Starting Kit** | **[set on platform]** - ICAIF 2026 Trading Agent Starter Kit |

**Description:** The live competition runs over the U.S. trading days from Oct 26 to Nov 6. Each
trading day, your agent connects to the official server, receives that day's observation, and
returns target weights before the **9:00 AM ET** cutoff; the official client submits automatically
and orders fill at the market open. The leaderboard updates once per trading day. Only your live
daily decisions determine the ranking.
