# Terms and Conditions

## Team participation

- A team may have at most **5 members**.
- A participant may **not belong to more than one registered team**. Violations may result in
  disqualification.
- Each team may make at most **10 validation submissions per day** during the development phase.

## Allowed LLMs

If your agent uses a large language model, it must be one of the models listed below, used at a
**pinned, dated version that you record** in your final materials.

- **Open-weight models:** Llama 4, Qwen3, DeepSeek-V3 / DeepSeek-R1, Gemma 3, Mistral Large.
- **Hosted API models:** OpenAI GPT-5 / GPT-5-mini, Anthropic Claude Opus 5 / Sonnet 5 /
  Haiku 4.5, Google Gemini 2.5 Pro / Flash, xAI Grok 4.

## News and external data

- The organizers **do not provide a news dataset.** Any news or other external information is
  **collected and processed by participants themselves.**
- External data must be **publicly and freely accessible to everyone.** Private, paid,
  proprietary, or non-public feeds are **not allowed.**
- Recommended public sources - a reference only, not a whitelist; you may use any other eligible
  public source: **Yahoo Finance** (financial news) and **SEC EDGAR** (company filings and
  disclosures). See the **Data & Submission** page for details.
- Participants are responsible for complying with each source's license and terms of use.

## No look-ahead

Each daily decision may use **only information that was publicly available at or before that day's
decision cutoff (9:00 AM ET).** Information that became available after the cutoff - including
later prices from the same day, or news published after the cutoff - may not be used.

## Final submission and reproducibility

- Code is submitted through our website after the live competition ends (before 23:59 ET, Nov 6 2026).
- There is a single **final-submission window** after the live phase ends and before the final
  leaderboard is published. To receive a final ranking, a team submits during this window its
  **code, the required model information, a video demo,** and any other materials needed for
  reproducibility.
- **Submitting the required final materials is a condition for final-ranking eligibility.** A team
  that does not submit within the window, or whose results the organizers cannot reproduce,
  **does not receive a final ranking.**
- The organizers receive these materials for **verification and reproducibility checking only;
  public release is not required.**
- Reproducibility is **not an evaluation metric.**

## Honor code

We strongly encourage all participants to compete with integrity and avoid any dishonest
practices. Leveraging information outside the allowed data sources, or manipulating the
competition infrastructure for an edge, is prohibited.

The organizers reserve the right, but not the obligation, to review submitted code and to
disqualify any contribution showing evidence of cheating. We respectfully request that you
refrain from any cheating.
